## 2. Common setup

- 10m preset dims: 6 layers, d=256, 8 heads, vocab 4096, tied embeddings, NoPE (this GPT has no position embedding).
- FineWeb-Edu tokens, seq 256, batch 16 (4,096 tokens/step), **10M tokens per arm** (2,442 steps), seed 0
  (a few seed-1 repeats), Apple MPS, fp32.
- AdamW lr 3e-3, betas (0.9, 0.95), wd 0, clip 1.0 (as `adam.py`), plus a WSD schedule: 100-step linear warmup,
  constant, linear decay to 10% over the last 20% of steps. (The older lab runs used constant LR, so absolute
  losses here are lower than e.g. `s-10m-k1-10m` 4.965; compare only within this file.)
- Eval: the harness's fixed 64×256-token validation batch, every 30 s of training time and at token marks.
- The GPU was shared with up to three other lab queues during these runs, so **all wall-time and tokens/s
  numbers are contaminated by contention** and only loss-per-token comparisons are reliable.

## 3. X4 design

- **MoE layer** (replaces every block's SwiGLU MLP): E experts, each a SwiGLU with hidden 512 (half the dense
  hidden 1024), **top-2**, so active MLP params equal the dense 10m preset (active ≈ 7.35M vs dense 7.34M total).
  Router: linear d→E, softmax; selection on logits + a loss-free balancing bias (DeepSeek-style sign update,
  1e-2 per step, no auxiliary loss); selected probabilities renormalised to sum 1.
- **Dispatch**: dense (all experts on all tokens, gated by a sparse gate matrix). Same values and gradients as
  sparse dispatch, much faster on MPS at E ≤ 8 than per-expert gathers (smoke: 8k → ~25k tok/s), but it means
  **MoE wall time is not representative** (an 8-expert layer costs 4× a 2-expert one here, not 1×).
- **Growth operator** (Expert Upcycling Def. 3.1, uniform copies): at a fixed fraction of the step budget, copy
  every expert m times, copy router rows, add U(−1e-3, 1e-3) to the copies' balancing biases, reset AdamW
  (fresh state, 50-step re-warmup). Gradient-utility allocation is **not** implemented (paper: uniform copying
  costs ~0.005–0.014 val loss at their scale).
- **Function-preservation finding (unit check, before any run)**: copying at fixed top-2 is **not**
  function-preserving for E ≥ 2 → 2E. Each token's two winners become the two copies of its single best expert, so the
  layer output becomes "expert 1 only" instead of "w1·e1 + w2·e2". On a random-init layer this changed the
  output by 97% (relative norm). It is exact only for dense→MoE (E=1 → 4), where all copies are identical anyway.
  The paper reports a small jump (1.32 → 1.38 at 7B) for the same reason. We therefore also test an
  **`offset` variant**: copies start 2.0 below their source in balancing bias, so the sources keep winning (exact at
  the event), and the balancer (1e-2/step) brings the copies in over ~200 steps.
- **Dense phase for (c)**: `E=1` is a dense SwiGLU of hidden 512 (a narrower dense model than the 10m preset);
  upcycling to 4 copies at top-2 is exact. `x4-dense512` is that narrow model trained alone; `x4-dense` is
  the stock 10m dense model (hidden 1024 = same active params as all MoE arms).
- **Document routing (EMO-style, causal)**: each sequence picks a group of G experts from the router applied to the
  running mean of its first 32 positions (position t < 32 uses positions 0..t only, so no future leak; unit-checked),
  then tokens route top-2 inside the group. G=4 (half the pool) and G=2 (pure per-document routing). Caveat: our
  training windows are random 256-token spans, not document starts.
- **Measured per growth event** (`g{i}pre/g{i}post/g{i}post20/100/300` in results JSON): eval loss just before,
  just after (same weights, before any step), and 20/100/300 steps later.

## 4. X5 design

- **GDN layer** (FLA `GatedDeltaNet` layout): q, k, v projections → causal depthwise conv(4) + SiLU; L2-normalised
  q, k; 2 heads, key dim 64, value dim 128; β = sigmoid(W_b x) (×2 with `neg=1`, allowing negative eigenvalues as
  in Olmo Hybrid); log α = −exp(A_log)·softplus(W_a x + dt_bias) (Mamba2 init); per-head RMSNorm of the output
  gated by SiLU(W_g x); output projection. Projections hold 8·d·H·dk = 4d², exactly the attention layer's size;
  +~3.6k conv/gate scalars per layer (hybrid 7.356M vs transformer 7.343M params, +0.2%).
- **Recurrence** S_t = α_t(I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ)S_{t−1} + β_t k_t v_tᵀ, o_t = S_tᵀ q_t/√dk, computed chunkwise (chunk 64,
  WY form after FLA's naive reference). The in-chunk triangular inverse uses the exact nilpotent product
  (I−M)(I+M²)(I+M⁴)…, so the whole layer is matmuls (MPS + autograd friendly), with 4 sequential chunk steps per sequence.
- **Validation** (float64 CPU): chunked output equals the token-by-token recurrence to ≤1e-15 (t = 37/64/256,
  β < 1 and β < 2, with padding); changing inputs after position 25 changes no earlier output (exactly 0).
- **Patterns** (6 layers, so exact 3:1 is impossible): `GGAGGA` (2:1, attention last like Olmo), `GGGGGA` (5:1,
  closest to Olmo's 7:1 ablation), `GGGGGG` (pure GDN), `AAAAAA` (plain transformer, same code path).
